30 × 40 Direct-Manipulation Matrix Sorter

Start dragging on a number and release to sort. Drag right → to sort its row ascending, left ← to sort its row descending, down ↓ to sort its column ascending, or up ↑ to sort its column descending. Drag diagonally down-right ↘ to sort both axes ascending or up-left ↖ to sort both axes descending.
